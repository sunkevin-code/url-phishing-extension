📖 Detection Layers

5-Layer Detection Architecture:

Layer 1 - URL Feature Rules (24 rules, 6-35 pts each)

  • IP-based hosts, @ symbol, multiple protocols, short URLs, suspicious TLDs, brand similarity, typosquatting, IDN homographs, etc.

Layer 2 - Page Content Rules (12 rules, 5-20 pts each)

  • Password forms, external form submission, hidden iframes, external scripts, HTTPS check, etc.

Layer 3 - Behavior Rules (2 rules, 10-15 pts each)

  • Rapid navigation, cross-domain forms

Layer 4 - ML Neural Network NEW

  • 38-dim features → 16 hidden layer → output probability (up to 60 pts)

Layer 5 - External APIs NEW

  • Google Safe Browsing (up to 60 pts), SSL certificate check (up to 20 pts)

Final Rating: ≥55 = High Risk | 28-54 = Medium Risk | 10-27 = Low Risk | <10 = Safe
